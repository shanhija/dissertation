# Fonts

The page's type, served with the game so that every player's browser shows the same book on the
LAN, with or without the internet. Both are under the SIL Open Font License 1.1 (the texts are
beside them), as packaged by Fontsource 5.3.0 (woff2, Latin subsets; EB Garamond also Latin
Extended).

| Font | Files | Use | Licence |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| IM Fell English and IM Fell English SC, by Igino Marini, from the Fell Types (cut in the 1670s and 1680s for Oxford's press) | `im-fell-english-*.woff2` | Titles, headings, buttons, what is printed large | `OFL-im-fell.txt` |
| EB Garamond (variable weight 400 to 800), by Georg Duffner and Octavio Pardo | `eb-garamond-*.woff2` | Everything read: text, labels, fields, the HUD | `OFL-eb-garamond.txt` |

Fetched from `cdn.jsdelivr.net/npm/@fontsource/im-fell-english@5.3.0`, `@fontsource/im-fell-english-sc@5.3.0`
and `@fontsource-variable/eb-garamond@5.3.0`.
